I am currently an Associate Professor and Ph.D. advisor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Tongji University. Previously, I was a postdoctoral research associate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and received the Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Tongji University in 2022.

Research Interests
======
My research interests include explainable AI, computer vision, and AI safety.
