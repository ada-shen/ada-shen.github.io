title: "Batch Normalization Is Blind to the First and Second Derivatives of the Loss"
collection: publications
permalink: /publication/AAAI2024-BN
date: 2024-02-01

This paper proves that batch normalization blocks the influence of the first- and second-order terms of the loss on earlier layers under a Taylor-series perspective.
