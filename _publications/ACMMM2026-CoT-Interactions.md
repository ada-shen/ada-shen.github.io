Abstract. This paper uses game-theoretic interactions to quantify inference patterns learned by vision-language models from chain-of-thought prompts, analyzing their complexity, adjacency, and cross-modal behavior.

Authors: Yaying Chen\*, **Wen Shen\***, Kelu Yao, Junxiao Xue, Chenghui Lv, Jin Wang, Chao Li.
